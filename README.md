During one of the many Carnatic music conversations within my family, I 'discovered' the theoretical idea of "Graha Bhedam". I was pleasantly surprised that this concept already had a name in Carnatic music. Truly grateful to be exposed to this rich artform.
Since then, I had been trying to identify the 'equivalent sounding ragas' of any raga I was listening to; but developing an intuition for this proved to be surprisingly hard. 
This simple project is an outcome of me trying to brute-force a way to help build that intuition.

Would love to connect with anyone also interested in carnatic music conversations.
